WHAT IS CLAPP-RT

CLAPP-RT is a policy research and training center under Cavite State University, established to serve as a platform for evidence-based policymaking, leadership development, and governance innovation.

VISION

To be the first Policy Center in Cavite renowned for multidisciplinary research, extension initiatives and policy analysis for a sustainable future.

MISSION

The Center shall strengthen research-based data analysis, policymaking and training focusing on economic issues, organizational development, inclusivity, sustainability, community development, gender equality, business and innovation, and international cooperation.

OBJECTIVES

-Conduct policy research and data-driven analysis
-Extend the product of research and help assess current policies and their implementation
-Provide training to LGUs, private sectors, and leaders
-Offer expert services related to policy advocacy which includes policy analysis, policy recommendation, and policy intervention
-Explore possible collaboration with local and international funding agencies
